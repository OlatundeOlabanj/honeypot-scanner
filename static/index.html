How It Works
💀

What Is a Honeypot?

A contract that lets you buy but blocks selling. Scammers wait for victims, then drain liquidity — funds trapped forever.

🔍

How We Detect It

We simulate real buy and sell transactions. If selling fails or incurs 90%+ tax, the token is flagged. We also check for blacklists, mintable supply, and proxy upgrades.

⚠️

Common Patterns

  • Owner-only sell (blacklist)
  • 90–100% hidden sell tax
  • Sweeper bots on deposits
  • Upgradeable proxy contracts
  • Freeze authority (Solana)
🛡

Stay Protected

  • Always scan before buying
  • Avoid tokens under 48h old
  • Never trust Telegram/TikTok hype
  • Only risk what you can lose
